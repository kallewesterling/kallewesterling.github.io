This is a now page: what I’d tell a friend I hadn’t seen in a year.

Where I am

I live in Madrid, and have for about a year. Before that I lived in Stockholm, New York City, Rio de Janeiro and Reading in the UK.

At work

I’m a Staff Developer Relations Engineer at Chainguard, wrapping up a busy 2026 on the conference circuit in Europe and the U.S.

  • Capture the flag season. In the past few weeks I’ve run Operation Lockdown in Paris and an advanced edition in Dresden, and Faithful by Default with Somerford in London. More about the games.
  • Learning Labs. Monthly live workshops on secure agent workflows. The next one, on 17 December, is about getting fewer CVEs into agent-built code.
  • Courses. Still writing courses and learning paths for Chainguard Courses.

On the side

I’m building small tools for the friction I hit in my own teaching:

  • session-pacer, for keeping live sessions on time.
  • errata and CodexMachina, for catching broken code examples in published courses and docs.
  • cvediff, for comparing the CVEs in two container images.

And one that goes back to my research roots: a data project on Swedish theatre history.
